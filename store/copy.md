## Description
Greyskull LP is a workout log for the Greyskull LP strength program. It tells you what to lift today, with which plates, and what to lift next time.

HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS
Each lift has two work sets. The last set is as many reps as you can do. Five or more reps adds weight. Ten or more adds double. Miss twice in a row and the lift drops 10 %. Greyskull LP does this for you after every workout.

WHAT YOU GET
• Today screen with the lifts, sets and weights for your next workout
• Plate maths: weights are the bar plus pairs of the plates you own, in kg or lb
• Edit any weight at any time, and the plan carries on from there
• Rest timer with a custom length and an alert when it ends
• Projections for where each lift is heading
• History and progress charts, with stalls and deloads marked
• Add your own exercises
• Minimalist mode for a plainer workout screen
• Home-screen widget with today's lifts
• Backup and restore, so a new phone keeps your history
• Other beginner programs built in, including Phrak's GSLP, 5x5 and full-body routines

NO ACCOUNT. NO TRACKING.
Your data stays on your phone. The app has no ads and sends nothing anywhere.
