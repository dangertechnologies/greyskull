Greyskull LP support

Email support@rudolfsson.dev and include your app version and phone model.

How do I move my history to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings and use Export backup and share it to Notes, Files or Messages. On the new phone, use Import backup and paste it or choose the file.

Can I change a weight?

Yes. Tap any weight and edit it. The plan carries on from the new number.

Why can't I pick any weight?

Weights are the bar plus pairs of the plates you own, so every weight can be loaded. Change your bar and plates in Settings.

kg or lb?

Both work. Change the unit in Settings.

How do I reset the app?

Settings, then Reset. This deletes the program, weights and history on this phone. Export a backup first.
