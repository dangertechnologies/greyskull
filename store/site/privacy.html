Greyskull LP privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Greyskull LP does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data stays on your phone

Your plan, weights and workout history are stored on your device only. The app sends none of it to the developer or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Backups

If you export a backup, it goes where you choose to share it. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Notifications

The rest-timer alert is a local notification created on your device. It does not use a server.

Links

Exercise pages link to technique videos and guides on YouTube and stronglifts.com. These open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies.

Updates

App updates are delivered through Expo's update service, which may receive your device's technical information (such as app version and platform) to choose the right update. It receives no workout data.
